# MarketBridge 3-Minute Demo Script

## Introduction

MarketBridge is a platform by BHAF that helps African women entrepreneurs become visible, trusted, and easier to support.

It brings business profiles, product listings, opportunities, ESG evidence, and role-based dashboards into one place.

The main idea is simple:

- entrepreneurs can show who they are and what they offer
- funders can find businesses that are more ready for support
- corporate partners can find suppliers
- BHAF can manage the ecosystem from one system

## Start With The Landing Page

Route: `/`

What to say:

"This is the public front page of MarketBridge. It introduces the platform, the story behind it, and the different kinds of users it supports."

What to show:

- the hero section

## Directory

Route: `/directory`

What it is:

The directory is where different women entrepreneurs are listed.

What you can do there:

- search for entrepreneurs
- filter by sector
- filter by country
- filter by readiness level
- view structured business profiles

What to say:

"This page helps funders, buyers, and partners discover different women entrepreneurs in a clean and structured way."

## Marketplace

Route: `/marketplace`

What it is:

This is where entrepreneur listings appear.

What you can do there:

- search for products and services
- filter by category
- open listings
- send an enquiry after login

What to say:

"The marketplace answers the question: what is this business actually offering?"

## Opportunities

Route: `/opportunities`

What it is:

This is the opportunity board.

What you can do there:

- view grants
- view funding opportunities
- view procurement opportunities
- apply after login

What to say:

"This page helps entrepreneurs move from visibility to access."

## Impact And ESG

Route: `/impact`

What it is:

This page shows the platform's impact story and ESG framework.

ESG means:

- Environmental
- Social
- Governance

What you can do there:

- show impact metrics
- show ESG pillars
- show how BHAF can present ecosystem results

What to say:

"This page is for showing outcomes, evidence, and reporting. It helps BHAF explain the value and impact of the entrepreneur network."

## Portals

What they are:

The portals are the private workspaces for each role after login.

Important note:

You can browse the public pages without logging in, but you cannot make requests, create listings, upload documents, or apply to opportunities until you log in as one of the platform roles.

## The 4 Main Roles

### 1. Women Entrepreneur

Who they are:

Women-led businesses joining the platform.

What they can do:

- build a business profile
- upload documents
- add ESG activity
- create listings
- apply to opportunities
- send and track enquiries

### 2. Funder

Who they are:

Investors, donors, and finance partners.

What they can do:

- discover entrepreneurs
- review readiness
- build pipelines
- look at impact information

### 3. Corporate Partner

Who they are:

Companies looking for suppliers or partners.

What they can do:

- browse the marketplace
- find suppliers
- review profiles
- run sourcing or procurement activity

### 4. Admin

Who they are:

BHAF team members managing the platform.

What they can do:

- review profiles
- review applications
- monitor activity
- manage the ecosystem
